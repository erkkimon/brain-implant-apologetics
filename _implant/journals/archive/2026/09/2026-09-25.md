# 2026-09-25

## Phase 1 done: 471 Finnish articles, no tokens spent

The conversion ran as pure machine time, exactly as the plan wanted: see
[the convert skill](../../../../skills/convert-apowiki-to-articles.md) for the
procedure and the gotchas. The result: **471 articles, 377 redirects resolved,
~2 500 internal links, 5 111 footnotes with their book citations intact,
44 topic pages built from ApoWiki's own categories, 0 ghosts, all henxels
hold.** Search already finds *Evolutiivinen argumentti naturalismia vastaan*
for a Finnish Plantinga query.

Things the data corrected: the redirect count was 377, not 156; and the
largest link cluster, `AW:S` (414), is not broken but missing. It is the
glossary in ApoWiki's project namespace, which the fetch never took. Topic
pages were not in the original sketch. They became necessary because 54
articles had no internal link at all, and weakening the grounding rule would
have hidden the problem rather than fixed it.

`bge-m3` turned out to be pulled and configured already; what was missing was
brainpick's vector extra, so search was quietly degrading to keywords.

## The MVP gate, and the wiki links that were already there

Tom set the order for the whole project: **get both implants to a minimum
lovable product first, then iterate** — because the full job takes days of
local-model time and a real share of subscription tokens, and a brain that is
already in use pays for the rest of the work. The plan is
[plans/mvp-then-iteration.md](../../../../plans/mvp-then-iteration.md), with the
canonical version in the theology implant, and the live queue in
[todo/open.md](../../../../todo/open.md).

What makes the gate reachable early is that the Finnish-first order is not a
compromise: the embedding model is multilingual, so Finnish pages answer
English queries. That rests on replacing the English-only `nomic-embed-text`
with `bge-m3` — a free model, and a phase-1 task rather than an afterthought,
because without it the premise is silently false.

**Tom's interlinking hypothesis, checked against the data.** He proposed
parsing the wiki links out of the exported articles algorithmically instead of
having a model invent them. Measured: **6 798 links in 845 of 848 files** — the
top targets are `AW:S` (415), Tapio Puolimatka (111), Alvin Plantinga (60),
then the category pages. So the initial graph comes from string parsing, and
the LLM is spent later on translation and judgement instead. 156 of the files
are `#REDIRECT` and should resolve to their targets rather than become pages.

One near-miss worth keeping: I first grepped `*.md` in `raw/apowiki-export/`
and got zero links, which looked like the hypothesis was wrong. The exports are
`.wiki` files. An empty glob is not a measurement.

The spine decision (book article per biblical book, chapters joined to it,
books bound to testaments, apocrypha outside the spine) is recorded in the
theology implant's journal for today; it binds here through federation, and
the `Luokka:Kirjat` corpus hangs off the book articles.
