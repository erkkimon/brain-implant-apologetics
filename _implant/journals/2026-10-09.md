## Batch 1 is done: Cosmic fine-tuning and Design theory landed

The last two design-theory articles are reviewed and landed in
[knowledge/en/](../knowledge/en/index.md), which completes batch 1's six.
Two subagents did one review each. Each followed the
[translate skill](../skills/translate-an-article.md)'s step 5: read the
current Finnish, translate the failed blocks by hand, then verify by script
that link targets, footnote references and HTML comments match the Finnish
exactly. I spot-checked their biggest fixes against the Finnish before
landing.

The 9B model's worst errors were inverted meanings:
* "Ei muodissa" (out of fashion) became "not due to fashion".
* Naturalists who *gave up* a claim became naturalists who *conceded* it.
* Design theory resembling methodological naturalism came out the other way
  round.
* "Kaikki klaavoja" (all tails) became "all heads".
* *Menninkäiset* (goblins) became angels.
* "Sata triljoonaa" came out as 10^14 instead of 10^20.

The model also added links the Finnish lacks; the link check catches those.
*Suunnitteluteoria* had six blocks that failed their checks and *Kosminen
hienosäätö* one, all translated by hand.

Landing re-pointed five English pages at the new translations. Batches 2–4
(34 articles) are next; the review recipe is in
[todo/open.md](../todo/open.md).

## 91 Wikipedia links now land where ApoWiki meant

I finished the audit of ApoWiki's direct Finnish-Wikipedia links, which was
started on [2026-10-02](archive/2026/10/2026-10-02.md). `WRONG_CONCEPT_INTERWIKI`
in `skills/tools/apowiki_convert.py` now has 80 hand-checked entries,
covering 91 links. For each one I read the target's lead against ApoWiki's
sentence:

* Disambiguation pages are resolved by context. *Locke* is John Locke
  (beside Spinoza and Leibniz), *Zenon* is the Stoic (beside Socrates and
  Epicurus), *Carnegie* is Lewis's Carnegie Medal, and *migraatio* is gene
  flow (Geenivirta), because the sentence lists it as a mechanism of
  hereditary variation.
* Typos are corrected: *Jacques Manod*, *Motreal*, *Teolgoia*.
* People and places with no Finnish article go to English Wikipedia:
  Westcott and Hort, Lanfranc, Robert M. Price, the Garden Tomb. The
  table gained `en:` targets for this.
* Two people have no page of their own. Freke and Gandy are named only
  together, for *The Jesus Mysteries*, so both go to that book. ApoWiki
  names Matti Saari as the Kauhajoki shooter, so he goes to the shooter
  section of that article.

Links to titles with parentheses, such as *Analogia (kielifilosofia)*,
exposed a bug in the converter's description extraction: it left a stray
")" in the text. Four existing descriptions had it too (*mutaatiot)*,
*Turnerin)*); the extraction now allows one level of parentheses in a
link target.

About 35 links point at pages that exist in no Wikipedia. Most are the
German authors of *Evoluutio – kriittinen analyysi*. They stay as wanted
links, and [todo/open.md](../todo/open.md) lists them.
