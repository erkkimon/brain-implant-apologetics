## Batch 2 prepared: God and the arguments

Batch 2 is the plan's second theme, ranked by inbound links like batch 1:
*jumala*, *jumalan olemassaolo*, the cosmological, kalam, ontological and
moral arguments, *Anselmin jumalatodistus*, *teismi*, *ihme*, *jumalan
kaikkivoipuus* and *hyvyys*, Thomas Aquinas and Antony Flew. That's 13
articles and about 15 500 words. *Alvin Plantinga* is the theme's most-linked
page but alone is 12 000 words, so it gets a run of its own.

Its glossary rows went in first, as the skill requires: 28 of them, so 120 of
413 terms now have English
([english.md](../knowledge/glossary/english.md)). 36 more terms were left
free on purpose and are listed at the end of that page: everyday words, and
stems that would catch other words (*per se* matched *perusteella sen*).

Doing this exposed a trap. `--dry-run` wrote its untranslated output to
`_temp/translations/`, where the batch runner and `apowiki_land.py` take
any file as a finished translation. It now writes nothing and lists the
terms still missing a row instead ([skill](../skills/translate-an-article.md)).
The batch 2 runner is queued behind batch 1.

## A model timeout no longer loses the article

The translation of *Tarkoituksellisuuspäättelyn logiikka* died after four
finished chunks. The shared CPU host was busy with another user's embedding
job, the fifth request passed the one-hour HTTP timeout, and the exception
discarded the whole article. A failed model call now fails only its chunk,
which is kept in Finnish and marked like any other failed check
([skill](../skills/translate-an-article.md)). A second runner,
`_temp/run-batch1b.sh`, waits for the first to finish and retries whatever
batch 1 is still missing.
