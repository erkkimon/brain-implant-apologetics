## The first wanted pages: Nelson, Denton, Discovery Institute

The shared host is saturated (the translation batch gets about a third of
the CPU it got yesterday), so this work doesn't need the local model. Three
design-theory targets ApoWiki linked to but never wrote now have Finnish
pages in [knowledge/pages/](../knowledge/pages/index.md):
[Paul Nelson](../knowledge/pages/paul-nelson.md),
[Michael Denton](../knowledge/pages/michael-denton.md) and
[Discovery Institute](../knowledge/pages/discovery-institute.md). Each claim
cites a pinned English Wikipedia revision.

They live in a folder of their own because `articles/` is regenerated
from the wikitext and would wipe them. The converter now reads each page's
`title` and `aliases` and links matching red links there: 10 links, three
spellings of the institute among them. The procedure is a new skill,
[Write a wanted page](../skills/write-a-wanted-page.md). The institute page
attributes the courts' and scientists' judgment instead of adopting
Wikipedia's "pseudoscientific" in its own voice.

A fourth page followed: [Älykkään suunnitelman idea](../knowledge/pages/älykkään-suunnitelman-idea-kirja.md),
the Finnish edition of Dembski's book. ApoWiki cites it in eleven articles,
so the page is grounded in those citations, each footnoted with its article
permalink, plus Open Library's record of the 1999 original.

## A model timeout no longer loses the article

The translation of *Tarkoituksellisuuspäättelyn logiikka* died after four
finished chunks. The shared CPU host was busy with another user's embedding
job, the fifth request passed the one-hour HTTP timeout, and the exception
discarded the whole article. A failed model call now fails only its chunk,
which is kept in Finnish and marked like any other failed check
([skill](../skills/translate-an-article.md)). A second runner,
`_temp/run-batch1b.sh`, waits for the first to finish and retries whatever
batch 1 is still missing.
