# 2026-10-01

## The 41 wiki tables are markdown tables now

The converter fenced every wiki table as raw `wikitext` so nothing was lost.
The cells inside were already converted by the later passes (links,
footnotes, bold), so only the table syntax was left. A new pass turns them
into markdown tables: headers from `!` rows, colspan padded, captions as an
italic line, continuation lines joined with `<br>`, empty trailing rows
dropped. Rowspan above 1 and nested tables would stay fenced, but none occur.
All 41 converted in 26 articles (the gematria letter table, the manuscript
comparison in *Uusi testamentti*, the entropy grid …). The page description
no longer picks up a table caption. The converter also reports no unknown
templates, so the "conversion degradation" item is closed. Recorded in
[the conversion skill](../skills/convert-apowiki-to-articles.md).

## Red links: a resolution bug, then Wikipedia fallbacks

The most-wanted red link, *Michael Behe* (30 links), was not missing at all:
the corpus has the article. The converter looked titles up lower-cased, and
the redirect *Michael behe* overwrote the article *Michael Behe* in that
table. It now resolves the way MediaWiki does (exact, then a capitalised first
letter, then case-insensitive with articles before redirects) and strips
U+200E marks, leading colons and `#section` first. 49 links came back, *Matti
Leisola*, *Arkeologia ja Raamattu* and *Luominen Raamatussa* among them.

For the rest, the new `apowiki_redlinks.py` looked all 584 targets up in the
Finnish Wikipedia: 345 hits, disambiguation pages excluded. I reviewed every
hit against the sentence it sits in and nulled 15. Bare years were one group.
Some were a different subject of the same name: *David Quinn* (a debater
here, a hockey player there), *perusryhmä* (a created kind here, topology
there), *Isä* (the Father of the Trinity). Two led to the wrong fallacy: ad
populum went to ad auctoritatem. The remaining 330 targets (396 links) now
link to Wikipedia and keep their red-link comment. 254 targets (273 links)
stay plain text, and they are the real list of pages to write ([todo](../todo/open.md)).
Recorded in [the conversion skill](../skills/convert-apowiki-to-articles.md).

## The 41 undefined glossary terms, decided

ApoWiki's articles link 41 terms to the glossary that it never defines (48
links). Until now they landed on the top of the glossary page. Each was
decided by hand and recorded in `GLOSSARY_FALLBACKS` in the converter (the
reasoning is in [the conversion skill](../skills/convert-apowiki-to-articles.md)):

- 10 go to a near-variant the glossary does define (*argumentoida* to
  *argumentointi*, *väitelause* to *propositio*, *falsifioitavuus* to
  *falsifikaatio* …).
- 6 go to an ApoWiki article (*hermeneutiikka*, *luonnonlaki*, *teisti* to
  *teismi*, *kanoniset evankeliumit* to *evankeliumit* …).
- 16 go to a Finnish Wikipedia page that exists, is not a disambiguation page
  and has the sense the article uses (*usko*, *monoteismi*, *trinitarismi* to
  *Kolminaisuusoppi*, *dimensio* to *Ulottuvuus* …).
- 9 become plain text (*konservatiivinen*, *leegio*, *teoreettinen*,
  *attribuutti* …). No page is about them in the sense used, and a link to
  the top of a 400-term glossary tells the reader nothing.

28 articles changed. The report's `missing_glossary_terms` is now empty.
