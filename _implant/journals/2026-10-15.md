## American spelling

Tom chose American spelling for the English pages. It is now
[a convention](../conventions/american-english.md), and a converter does the
work: `skills/tools/apowiki_american.py`, which
`skills/tools/apowiki_land.py` runs on every page it
lands. On the 53 landed pages it changed 153 lines in 33 files, mostly
*towards*, *criticised*, *behaviour*, *favourable*, *sceptic* and
*programme*. A script compared each page before and after: link targets,
HTML comments and footnotes are all unchanged. The first run also converted
a title, Antony Flew's *… In Defence Of …*: a list bullet's `*` had been
read as the start of the italics. The converter now requires italics to
start and end on a non-space, and the title keeps *Defence*.

batch 5, so `_temp/run-batch6.sh` started at once.
