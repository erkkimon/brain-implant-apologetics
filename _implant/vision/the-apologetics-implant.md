# The apologetics implant

Apologetics is the rational defense of Christian belief against non-Christian
views. This implant makes that body of knowledge available to agents and
humans in a format that carries its sources and its evidence grading — not
as a pile of assertions, but as a navigable, citable, replayable knowledge
graph.

It is founded on [ApoWiki](https://apowiki.fi), a Finnish-language
apologetics wiki founded ~2005, GFDL-1.3-or-later, with 20 years of
contributor history. The import is faithful: every article's edit history is
transcribed into git commits attributed to the real ApoWiki contributors on
the real dates, so GFDL's authorship-preservation requirement is met
honestly and the brainpick web UI shows the history per article.

The stem implant's conventions — [written to be
public](../conventions/written-to-be-public.md), [every claim carries its
receipt](../conventions/every-claim-carries-its-receipt.md), [evidence before
authority](../conventions/evidence-before-authority.md), and [credence is a
model not a measurement](../conventions/credence-is-a-model-not-a-measurement.md)
— are inherited, because apologetics is epistemically the same
problem in a different domain: claims that must be sourced, graded, and
separated from the authority of those who made them. The Bayesian appraisal
tool, shared via the federated common implant, applies to the joints where
evidence runs out and interpretation begins.

The scope boundary with the theology implant is deliberate: apologetics
defends against views *outside* the faith; theology defends one Christian
view against *other* Christian views. The Bible itself is shared ([the scope
convention](../conventions/apologetics-vs-theology-scope.md)).

How the vision becomes work is decided in [the MVP
plan](../plans/mvp-then-iteration.md), and what the import produced is
browsable from the [article index](../knowledge/index.md).
