# English renderings of ApoWiki's glossary terms

Each row fixes how one [Sanasto](sanasto.md) term is written in English. A
translated article must use exactly this rendering, inflected as English
grammar needs, wherever the Finnish text uses the term. The rule and its
reasoning are in [Translate an article](../../skills/translate-an-article.md).

How a row gets here:

* The **English** column is the established term in English-language
  philosophy of religion and apologetics, where one exists, for example the
  entries of the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/).
  It is not a literal gloss of the Finnish.
* The **sense** column says which of ApoWiki's definitions it renders, when
  the Finnish word has several. ApoWiki's struck-through senses are never
  the one rendered.
* A term without a row has no fixed rendering yet. The translator may
  render it freely, and the batch that first meets it adds the row.
  `apowiki_glossary.py` reports how many terms are still missing.

The first rows cover the terms articles link to most often (counted from the
converted articles on 2026-09-27). They are a starting point chosen by the
agent, not rulings. Correct any row freely: this page is the place to do it,
and the next translation batch picks the correction up.
