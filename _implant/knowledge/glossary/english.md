# English renderings of ApoWiki's glossary terms

Each row fixes how one [Sanasto](sanasto.md) term is written in English. A
translated article must use exactly this rendering, inflected as English
grammar needs, wherever the Finnish text uses the term. The rule and its
reasoning are in [Translate an article](../../skills/translate-an-article.md).

How a row gets here:

* The **English** column is the established term in English-language
  philosophy of religion and apologetics, where one exists, for example the
  entries of the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/).
  It is not a literal gloss of the Finnish.
* The **sense** column says which of ApoWiki's definitions it renders, when
  the Finnish word has several. ApoWiki's struck-through senses are never
  the one rendered.
* A term without a row has no fixed rendering yet. The translator may
  render it freely, and the batch that first meets it adds the row.
  `apowiki_glossary.py` reports how many terms are still missing.

The first rows cover the terms articles link to most often (counted from the
converted articles on 2026-09-27). They are a starting point chosen by the
agent, not rulings. Correct any row freely: this page is the place to do it,
and the next translation batch picks the correction up.

## Left free on purpose

Batch 2 (God and the arguments, 2026-10-02) met these terms and gave them
no row ([journal](../../journals/archive/2026/10/2026-10-02.md)). They are either everyday
Finnish whose English the model already gets right, or their stem catches
unrelated words, so a row would fail correct chunks: *hyvä*, *käsite*,
*halu*, *tahto*, *tarkoitus* (also *tarkoittaa*), *kohtalo* (also *kohta*),
*määritys*, *ilmiö*, *järki*, *kosmos*, *olio*, *annettu*, *arvo*,
*arvostus*, *eliö*, *laji*, *normaali*, *absoluutti*, *absurdi* (also
*absurdum*), *negaatio*, *konteksti*, *humanisti* (also *humanismi*),
*monismi* (also *monista*), *agenda* (also *agentti*), *per se* (also
*perusteella sen*), *apologi*, *apologia*, *apologeetta*, *apologeetti*,
*apologeetikko* (one stem, several English words), *argumentointi*,
*argumentaatio*, *teoreema* (also *teoreettinen*), *ID*, *abiogeneettinen*,
*olevainen*.

Batch 3 (atheism, 2026-10-02) left these free for the same reasons: *eliö*,
*laji*, *humanisti*, *analyysi* (also *analyyttinen*, "analytic"),
*tyranni* (also *tyrannia*, "tyranny"), *verifioida* (also
*verifioitavuus*, "verifiability"), *totuudellisuus*, *professuuri*,
*emeritus-*, *emerita-*, *emeritusprofessori* ("emeritus professor" and
"professor emeritus" are both right).

Batch 5 (Jesus and the Bible, 2026-10-09) left these free: *rekonstruktio*
and *rekonstruoida* (one stem, "reconstruction" and "reconstruct"),
*variantti* (a textual variant or a mere variant), *impulssi*, *symboli*,
*teoreema*, *tyranni*, *ilmiö* and *termi*: everyday words in these
articles, which the model renders well.

Batch 6 (philosophy and worldview, 2026-10-15) left these free:
*koherentti*, *postuloida*, *anomalia*, *algoritmi*, *primitiivinen*,
*optimismi*, *kollega*, *järjellinen*, *argumentatiivinen* and *abstrakti*
are everyday words the model renders well. *deduktio* has no row because
its stem also catches *deduktiivinen*, whose English ("deductive") does not
contain "deduction".

Batch 4 (evolution, 2026-10-02) left these free: *termi*, *elementti*,
*fakta*, *täsmennys*, *intuitiivinen*, *retorinen* and *orgaaninen* are
everyday words the model renders well. *surkastuma* has no row because its
stem also catches the adjective *surkastunut* ("vestigial"), which a noun
rendering would fail. The row for *surkastunut elin* carries the sense.
*synteesi* has none either, because its stem catches *synteettinen*,
whose English ("synthetic") does not contain "synthesis".
