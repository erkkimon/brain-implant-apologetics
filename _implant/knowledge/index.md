# Knowledge

Semantic memory — evergreen concept docs, one concept per kebab-case page.
Ground every claim inline (a journal entry, a link, or say it is assumed).
When a concept becomes a procedure that works, distil it into `../skills/`
and point here to the skill.

## Contents

* [Raamatun kirjat](books/index.md) — the book spine: 66 books under their
  two testaments, each bound to the theology implant, which owns the spine.
* [Sanasto](glossary/index.md) — ApoWiki's official glossary (413 terms) and
  the fixed English rendering of each term, which is the translation contract.
* [Aihepiirit](topics/index.md) — topic pages built from ApoWiki's own
  categories; every article links back to its topics.
* `articles/` — the 471 ApoWiki articles, converted mechanically from the
  wikitext in `../raw/apowiki-export/` by the
  [convert skill](../skills/convert-apowiki-to-articles.md). They are the
  Finnish source of truth.
* [English](en/index.md) — translated articles, one per Finnish page under
  the same file name, added batch by batch per the
  [translation skill](../skills/translate-an-article.md).
