# Pages written here

Pages ApoWiki linked to but never wrote, written for this implant in Finnish.
Unlike `../articles/`, these are not converted from the wiki and the converter
never overwrites them: it reads each page's `title` and `aliases` and links
the matching red links here. Every claim cites a pinned source revision. The
procedure is [Write a wanted page](../../skills/write-a-wanted-page.md).

## Design theory

* [Discovery Institute](discovery-institute.md): the think tank whose
  Center for Science and Culture is the hub of the design movement.
* [Älykkään suunnitelman idea](älykkään-suunnitelman-idea-kirja.md): the
  Finnish edition of Dembski's *Intelligent Design* (2002), which ApoWiki
  cites in eleven articles.
* [Guillermo Gonzalez](guillermo-gonzalez.md): astronomer, co-author of
  *The Privileged Planet*.
* [Jonathan Wells](jonathan-wells.md): biologist and theologian, author
  of *Icons of Evolution*.
* [Jay W. Richards](jay-w-richards.md): philosopher, the other co-author of
  *The Privileged Planet*.
* [Michael Denton](michael-denton.md): the biochemist whose 1985 book set
  Behe doubting.
* [Evolution: A Theory in Crisis](evolution-a-theory-in-crisis-kirja.md):
  that book (1985), with its reception; cited in four articles.
* [Paul Nelson](paul-nelson.md): philosopher of science, design theorist
  and young-earth creationist.
* [Myytti apinaihmisistä](myytti-apinaihmisistä-kirja.md): the Finnish
  edition of Lubenow's book on hominid fossils (2005), from Leisola's
  Datakirjat.
* [Phillip Johnson](phillip-johnson.md): law professor, author of *Darwin
  on Trial*, a founder of the design movement.
* [Darwin on Trial](darwin-on-trial-kirja.md): Johnson's 1991 book that
  launched the design movement, cited in six articles.
* [The Design Inference](the-design-inference-kirja.md): Dembski's 1998
  Cambridge book that sets out the explanatory filter, cited in nine articles.
* [Agents Under Fire](agents-under-fire-kirja.md): Menuge's 2004 book on
  materialism and the rationality of science; a catalogue-only page.
* [No Free Lunch](no-free-lunch-kirja.md): Dembski's sequel on specified
  complexity and evolutionary algorithms (2001–2002), cited in six articles.
* [Evoluution maailmankuva](evoluution-maailmankuva-kirja.md): the Finnish
  edition of Johnson's *Defeating Darwinism by Opening Minds* (2000).
* [Stanley Jaki](stanley-jaki.md): Benedictine priest and physicist,
  historian of science and Christianity.
