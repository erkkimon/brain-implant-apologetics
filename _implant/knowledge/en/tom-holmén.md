# Tom Holmén

*Topics: [People](../topics/henkilöt.md), [Exegesis](../topics/eksegetiikka.md), [Jesus](../topics/jeesus.md).*

**Tom Holmén** (b. 1963) is a New Testament [exegesis](../articles/eksegetiikka.md) [docent](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/dosentti) at [Åbo Akademi](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Åbo_Akademi) and Finland's most internationally prominent Jesus researcher. Holmén earned his doctorate at Åbo Akademi with a dissertation on Jesus and Jewish covenant thinking, titled Jesus and Jewish covenant thinking.[^1] Holmén leads Åbo Akademi's research group The Historical Jesus Workshop.
