# New Atheism

*Topics: [Atheism](../topics/ateismi.md).*

**New Atheism** is a branch of [atheism](ateismi.md) closely bound up with many features associated with traditional [religions](uskonnon-määritelmä.md), especially the active spreading of its own world picture. The line between New Atheism and traditional atheism is blurred, but those considered new atheists are generally atheists who strive to convert theists into atheists. According to Timo Tiainen, a characteristic of the new atheists' proclamation is the idea that religion is the source of evil.[^1] On this definition, new atheists make up 4% of the Finnish population, according to a study published by the Church Research Center in 2007.[^2]

### Conversion

New atheists actively strive to convert people. Lasse Pylkki said the following in [Vapaa Ajattelija](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapaa-ajattelijain_liitto) <!-- red link: Vapaa Ajattelija -->:

> One must try to convert those who have not yet converted.[^3]

Most such bodies, such as [eroakirkosta.fi](http://www.eroakirkosta.fi), are run by volunteers.[^4] New atheists have begun to organize camps for children; one example is the children's summer camp in Somerset organized with Richard Dawkins's backing.[^5]

### Criticism

It is common for new atheists to interpret [scientific](../glossary/sanasto.md#tiede) results in a very one-sided manner and to ignore [theistic arguments](teismi.md) [systematically](../glossary/sanasto.md#systemaattinen). Their argumentation rests mainly on appeals to images and impressions and on a convenient selection of scientific results. Typical new atheists include [Richard Dawkins](richard-dawkins.md), [Daniel Dennett](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Dennett), [Sam Harris](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Harris), and [Christopher Hitchens](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Hitchens). In Finland, similar argumentation is represented, among others, by many [freethinkers](../articles/vapaa-ajattelijat.md) and to some extent by [Esko Valtaoja](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esko_Valtaoja).

Professor [Tapio Puolimatka](../articles/tapio-puolimatka.md) has examined this phenomenon and its background in his Kotimaa article [Uusateismin tunnekieli](tapio-puolimatka-uusateismin-tunnekieli.md). Public attention has also been drawn to it by the emeritus professor of ecumenics [Tuomo Mannermaa](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuomo_Mannermaa) on the eKotimaa site[^6], as well as by the Bishop of Helsinki [Eero Huovinen](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eero_Huovinen) in the magazine [Kanava](http://www.helsinginhiippakunta.evl.fi/piispa/helsingin_hiippakunnan_piispat/eero_huovinen/puheenvuoroja/?x20117=31485).

According to several statements by the freethinkers, religious freedom is not sufficiently realized in Finland,[^7] but according to a study conducted in 2000, only 2% of Finns feel that religious freedom is poorly realized.[^8]
