# God

*Topics: [God](../topics/jumala.md), [Theism](../topics/teismi.md).*

The word god can refer to many kinds of [supernatural beings](../glossary/sanasto.md#yliluonnollinen-olento); [Hinduism](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/hindulaisuus), for example, has many [gods](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/hindulaiset_jumalat), but no personal, sovereign creator God. This article deals with the creator God of the Abrahamic religions.

### The existence of God

*Main article: [The existence of God](jumalan-olemassaolo.md)*

Several arguments have been put forward for the existence of God, but none of them is irrefutable in the sense that it could not be sidestepped from some philosophical point of view. The proofs of God's existence include the [ontological argument](ontologinen-argumentti.md), the [cosmological argument](kosmologinen-argumentti.md), the [teleological argument](teleologinen-argumentti.md), the [moral argument](moraalinen-argumentti.md) and the [existentialist argument](pascalin-peliteoria.md).

### A God of the gaps?

*Main article: [God of the gaps](../articles/aukkojen-jumala-v2.md)*

Explaining things by God is sometimes regarded as a fallacy. The charge is then an [appeal to ignorance](../articles/argumentum-ad-ignorantiam.md), although in reality these arguments are based on knowledge.
