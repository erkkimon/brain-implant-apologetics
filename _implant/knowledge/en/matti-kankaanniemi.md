# Matti Kankaanniemi

*Topics: [People](../topics/henkilöt.md), [Exegesis](../topics/eksegetiikka.md), [Jesus](../topics/jeesus.md).*

*Image: Matti Kankaanniemi vammalassa.jpg*

**Matti Kankaanniemi** ([ThD](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teologian_tohtori), [MSc Econ.](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yliopistolliset_tutkinnot_Suomessa)) is a Jesus researcher at [Åbo Akademi](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Åbo_Akademi). He wrote his doctoral dissertation in New Testament exegesis, on the account of the *guards at the tomb* in the [Gospel of Matthew](../books/mat.md).[^1]
