# Antony Flew

*Topics: [People](../topics/henkilöt.md), [Atheism](../topics/ateismi.md), [Theism](../topics/teismi.md).*

**Antony Flew** (11 February 1923 – 8 April 2010) was a British philosopher. Antony Flew is known above all for formulating the principle that [atheism](../articles/ateismi.md) is the default assumption, which should be given up only for sufficiently good reasons.[^1] If a [theist](teismi.md), that is, a believer in God, cannot offer such reasons, atheism holds its ground as the only reasonable starting assumption. Atheism is thus "innocent until proven guilty". In the early 2000s, however, he announced that he had abandoned atheism and become a [deist](../glossary/sanasto.md#deismi).[^2]

It is specifically the modern variants of the teleological argument that Flew finds intellectually convincing enough that, on their basis, he considers it justified to give up atheism and adopt deism instead. Flew points out that Darwin, in his theory of evolution, does not even claim to explain the origin of life, but only the mechanisms that govern the development of organisms.

> It seems to me that Richard Dawkins constantly overlooks what Darwin himself says in the fourteenth chapter of his *Origin of Species*. There he points out that his whole argument begins with a being that is capable of reproduction. This is the product of evolution that a truly comprehensive theory of evolution ought to explain. Darwin himself was well aware that he had not offered such an explanation. It now seems to me that more than fifty years of DNA research have provided material for a new and enormously powerful argument for intelligent design. (Flew; quoted in Habermas 2005.)

Since Flew believes in the existence of God on the basis of the teleological argument, he regards his book *God and Philosophy*, which was built on atheistic thinking, as outdated, fit now only as a historical document with no direct bearing on current debates.

Flew considered the [resurrection of Jesus](../articles/jeesuksen-ylösnousemus.md) the best-attested [miracle](ihme.md) in the world[^3] – but still did not consider it well enough attested to believe in it, even after he had become a deist.[^4]
