# Darwin's Black Box (book)

*Topics: [Books](../topics/kirjat.md), [Design theory](../topics/suunnitteluteoria.md), [Theory of evolution](../topics/evoluutioteoria.md).*


**Darwin's Black Box: The Biochemical Challenge to Evolution** (DBB) is Michael Behe's first book on [design-theoretical](suunnitteluteoria.md) matters. In the book Behe presents his idea of "[irreducible complexity](../articles/palautumaton-monimutkaisuus.md)". Unlike a typical creationist book, Darwin's Black Box states that the ideas of a common ancestor of humans and apes, as well as of an old universe and an old earth, were valid.[^1] The book can be regarded as the most important work on design theory,[^2] and by 2008 it had sold more than 250,000 copies.[^3] [The List Universe](http://listverse.com) named Darwin's Black Box one of the 10 worst books in the world.[^4]

Darwin's Black Box is about the [theory of evolution](evoluutioteoria.md), "which the discoveries of biochemistry have forced to stretch to its limits".[^5] In the book Behe [argues](../glossary/sanasto.md#argumentointi) that the biologists of Darwin's time did not actually know what seeing, moving, eating, breathing and the other fundamental bodily functions were based on, and that because life was thought to be simple, [abiogenesis](../articles/abiogeneesi.md) was not considered a problem either.[^6]

### Contents of the book

Chapters 1 and 2 shed light on Behe's insight, which he calls *[irreducible complexity](../articles/palautumaton-monimutkaisuus.md)*. Chapters 3-7 describe the machines made of molecules that are the basis of life and that shatter the notion that life is simple. Chapter 8 discusses and criticizes [evolutionary-mechanistic](perinnöllisen-muuntelun-mekanismit.md) explanations for the origin of [molecular machines](../articles/molekyylikone.md). In chapters 9-11 Behe presents his own explanation for the origin of molecular machines.
