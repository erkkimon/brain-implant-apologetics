# The existence of God

*Topics: [God](../topics/jumala.md), [Arguments](../topics/argumentit.md).*

<!-- Lähtökohdat ja käsitteiden määritelmät -->

### Can the existence of God be proved?

The existence of God can never be proved conclusively. Not even the existence of this text can be proved conclusively. Only the existence of the subject can be proved, because proving the existence of anything else not contained in the subject requires as a premise, for example, that the human senses are at least to some degree reliable. Although the existence of God cannot be proved conclusively, convincing evidence can be presented for it. Although in everyday language one might say that the existence of God can be proved, it would be more precise to put it, for example, like this: "one can become convinced of the existence of God".

### Arguments for the existence of God

[Arguments](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/argumentti) put forward for the existence of God are called proofs of God's existence. The first significant proofs of God's existence were formulated by [Aristotle](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristoteles),[^1] and they played a central role especially in [medieval](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keskiaika) <!-- red link: keskiaika --> [scholastic](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skolastiikka) <!-- red link: skolastiikka --> [theology](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teologia) <!-- red link: teologia -->.[^1] According to [Tapio Puolimatka](../articles/tapio-puolimatka.md), the proofs of God's existence do not prove the existence of God irrefutably,[^2] so when speaking of individual proofs it is perhaps more correct to speak of them as arguments. What matters, then, is that the arguments are cumulative: often the conclusion of a proof of God's existence can be stated as "on the grounds presented, the existence of God is probable", and the more arguments that conclusion follows from, the more probable the existence of God can be considered.

The most significant proofs of God's existence are

* the [ontological argument](ontologinen-argumentti.md)
* the [cosmological argument](kosmologinen-argumentti.md)
* the [teleological argument](teleologinen-argumentti.md)
* the historical argument <!-- red link: historiallinen argumentti -->
* the [moral argument](moraalinen-argumentti.md)
* the [argument from consciousness](../articles/argumentti-tietoisuudesta.md)
