# Jeesus (book)

*Topics: [Books](../topics/kirjat.md), [Jesus](../topics/jeesus.md).*

**Jeesus** (*Jesus*) is a popular non-fiction book written by [exegesis](../articles/eksegetiikka.md) [docent](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/dosentti) [Tom Holmén](tom-holmén.md) that examines the person known as [Jesus of Nazareth](jeesus-nasaretilainen.md) from the [perspective of historical research](jeesuksen-historiallisuus.md). According to its preface, the book "should not be regarded as a devotional book or the like", since "the book deals with the so-called historical Jesus".[^1]
